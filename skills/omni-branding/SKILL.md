# Omni branding

For [dimensional comparison treatments](../omni-design-systems/references/dimensional-visualization-craft.md),
translate the retained style answer into materials, light, type and contrast while
preserving semantic colors. Futuristic need not mean neon or dark mode; official
asset authority still does not approve fonts, engines or imports.

For new products, follow the [data-first workflow](../omni-artifact-accelerator/references/data-first-product-workflow.md):
retain volunteered brand preferences at intake, conduct artifact/brand discovery
after the A–C analytical narrative, help distinguish the three viable concepts,
then resolve assets and visual identity for the user's selected option. Existing
brand authorization and focused corrections do not require repeat interviews.

For selected spatial work, extend the existing brand contract with [procedural material intent](../omni-3d-modeler/references/procedural-craft.md): structural/focal materials, roughness, texture scale, system-font fallback and logo placement. Reuse the approved company website and exact assets. Brand permission does not approve a font loader, remote image decoder or engine. Preserve analytical palette meaning under lighting and retain a clear legend/plain-value route. Do not force branded texture imagery into every scene.

Before runtime or tool use, follow the shared [package-use policy](../omni-artifact-accelerator/references/dependency-policy.md): no unapproved package installation/import or skill activation, and no Blender code or execution. Source review is separate from execution authority.

Own the brand interview, decision record, and consistent application across selected surfaces. Reuse accepted answers; ask only for consequential missing or conflicting choices. A generic task does not authorize changing organization-wide themes.

For substantial new design, material redesign, explicit craft feedback or deliberate practice, apply the [curriculum role route](../omni-design-systems/references/experience-curriculum.md#role-routes) for observation, art direction and identity. Demonstrate selected assets and brand character together through type, layout, density, imagery and behavior in the existing specimen, using [art-direction recipes](../omni-design-systems/references/experience-patterns.md#art-direction-recipes) as adaptable starting points while keeping semantic colors independent of decoration.

Use the [shared experience brief](../omni-design-systems/references/experience-brief.md#separate-behavior-from-treatment) to distinguish required identity and legibility from engagement-specific composition, typography, density and imagery. Decide the task and brand rationale before optional reference retrieval unless the user chose the reference. Retain observed qualities, proposed treatments and user decisions separately; an asset or recipe does not mandate a hero, card system or imagery. Hand over loading/fallback tradeoffs with the selected design.

## Inputs and interview

Use the shared [product-intent intake](../omni-artifact-accelerator/references/product-intent.md)
before opening another interview. Reuse no-logo, no-research and supplied-brand
decisions for the questions they resolve. Branding owns identity/assets; return
their product implications to the PO without restarting audience/outcome discovery.

Read the existing engagement brief, brand guidelines/assets, prior accepted theme, target surfaces, audience, and deployment scope before asking questions. Use [the interview and contract](references/brand-contract.md) to consolidate choices, and [saved design intent](../omni-artifact-accelerator/references/design-intent.md) to retain required versus optional decisions, their origin and unresolved/deferred obligations. A website answer alone cannot resolve the logo choice. For a standalone request, establish the intended brand and target first. A brand owner's approved source overrides inspiration; use a public site as evidence of observed styling, not proof of licensing or formal brand approval.

For a branded deliverable, ask for the missing company website and logo choice in the smallest relevant question batch: an approved logo file/URL, or authorization to source it from the official website. Reuse prior answers and sourcing authorization; record an explicit no-logo choice when requested. Review only relevant authorized pages for the selected logo/wordmark, colors, typography and imagery, recording source URLs and proposed theme implications. Reconcile observations with approved guidelines and existing Omni theme choices; keep unresolved fonts, assets and permissions visible. Use the bounded [asset manifest](references/brand-contract.md#purposeful-asset-manifest) for selected reusable assets; research references and eligible assets stay distinct. This is not an automated crawler or permission to collect every image.

For migrations, use the presentation and persona facets in [behavior coverage](../omni-qa-analyst/references/migration-behavior.md). Keep observed source formatting separate from the proposed brand treatment, preserving status/category meanings, units, number formats, and audience differences. Reuse accepted brand answers across peer handoffs; a model return does not reset theme decisions or approve a branding change.

When sample data needs customer-themed names or geography, use [demo presentation](references/demo-presentation.md). Distinguish original source, verified customer and authorized fictional modes; reuse existing decisions. Logo/website permission is separate from alias permission. Keep complete exact-identity maps, original disclosure and numerical invariants, and identify verified, approximate or manually invented locations without claiming business equivalence.

## Work

When branding controls, use [semantic control tokens](../omni-design-systems/references/control-craft.md#semantic-tokens-without-a-house-style) with the existing brand/design mapping. Preserve focus, selection, disabled/busy and status meaning while choosing the engagement's type, geometry, density and emphasis. Brand accent is not a universal selected/success color; shared tokens support distinct compositions rather than prescribe a house style. The relevant [focused lesson](../omni-design-systems/references/experience-curriculum.md#focused-control-practice) is optional practice.

Honor an explicit website omission or supplied-authority decision using the [saved website choice](../omni-artifact-accelerator/references/design-intent.md#one-short-interview-persistent-decisions). Keep its origin, affected surfaces and actual decision reference. A supplied logo alone is not that decision; an explicit no-research choice is not permission to infer an official website or omit other brand obligations.

1. Identify the authority for logos, color, typography, and tone; retain asset provenance and unresolved rights. Record the specific selected logo/variant, source, loading path and expected placement using [the logo handoff](references/brand-contract.md#logo-decision-and-handoff). Do not silently replace a requested brand logo with a generic monogram. A temporary placeholder must be labeled and unresolved; optional missing styling choices need not stop provisional design.
2. Define semantic colors and categorical/sequential/diverging chart palettes separately. Preserve category identity across charts and filters. Brand accent does not automatically mean success, loss, risk, or selected state.
3. Establish type roles, available weights, loading source, licensing confirmation, fallback, and numerical behavior. Verify the target's font controls and runtime network policy before promising a custom font.
4. Read [Omni capabilities](../omni-artifact-accelerator/references/omni-capabilities.md). Keep application theme, dashboard/document theme, and App CSS bindings distinct. Produce a field-by-field mapping with target scope, current value if known, candidate value, evidence, and unsupported gaps.
5. Qualify selected assets for their [actual display role](references/brand-contract.md#purposeful-asset-manifest): inspect retained dimensions/pixels when permitted, intended size/crop, historical context, loading route and assembled byte budget with engineering. Official provenance does not make a blurred placeholder or small portrait suitable for a large feature. Record unobserved suitability instead of claiming a visual check.
6. Demonstrate the brand on a representative screen: translate identity into type relationships, spacing, content grouping, asset roles and action vocabulary, not only colors. Use [visual craft](../omni-design-systems/references/visual-craft.md) for substantial new work or redesign. Give shared roles to [design systems](../omni-design-systems/SKILL.md), then inspect the actual app/dashboard at representative density and every supported appearance. Use [design QA](../omni-design-systems/references/design-qa.md), including contrast, focus, chart distinctions, font fallback, and logo legibility.

## Outputs and boundaries

Return the versioned brand contract, compact unanswered-question list, theme mapping, asset references, representative preview, and requirement-linked QA evidence. Ensure the current per-surface design selection points to this brand revision and selected asset bytes; preserve older records. Reuse a single specimen for the existing concept loop rather than adding another exercise. Distinguish approved brand choices, observed source styling, and recommendations.

Theme import/save behavior and organization/default/bulk scope must be checked before an authorized write. A design request alone does not authorize applying, replacing, or publishing a shared theme. Stop the affected asset or setting for a conflicting usage restriction, unresolved action scope or unsupported loading path; continue unaffected design. Do not request authorization again when the user has already authorized sourcing/use within this scope. Verify the selected logo is actually visible in the target; palette checks establish neither brand identity nor full accessibility conformance.

For Looker migrations, share the brand contract across the selected dashboard and App without changing metric semantics. Reuse the company website URL and approved assets already supplied; ask for them when missing. Track source identity, approved improvements, image/font origin and permission separately. See [the Looker run guide](../omni-artifact-accelerator/references/looker-visual-run.md).

For Hex, use [the shared experience treatment](../omni-app-designer/references/hex-experience-mapping.md#brand-composition-narrative-and-motion). Reconcile supplied themes, generated styles and imagery with the company website and approved assets. Keep their provenance/rights and observed-versus-approved status; reuse one brand contract across both targets without assuming identical theme controls.
