# Scoped specialist assignments

Use these responsibility profiles under the existing orchestrator. They are not
four new mandatory agents. Keep sequential or small work with the lead; delegate
only independent bounded scopes and avoid overlapping writes. Each task packet
uses the existing task-routing contract and exact source pins, scope IDs, stage,
outputs, acceptance criteria, unknowns and dependencies.

| Profile | Existing role | Inputs and responsibility | Reviewable handoff |
|---|---|---|---|
| Acquisition and inventory | `microstrategy-migrator` at discovery | Pinned selected source, original-file crosswalk and independent enumeration. Distinguish definitions, instance work and exports. | Immutable capture, object/placement/dependency map, inaccessible and unsupported obligations; no candidate code. |
| Dashboard and document interpretation | `artifact-translator`, then `dashboard-designer` or `app-designer` at the permitted stage | Chapters/pages, layout, selectors, navigation, conditional formatting, pixel-perfect and export obligations. | Source behavior matrix and target adaptation decisions. Screen design waits for D/E; do not promise automatic pixel-perfect conversion. |
| Semantic and analytical behavior | `data-analyst` with `bi-engineer` and `backend-handoff` | Attribute forms, metric levels/conditions, prompt/filter stages, security, raw/formatted results, grain and freshness. | Traceable metric expectations, approved Omni reuse or scoped DMA requests, unresolved expressions and population rules. |
| Independent migration QA | `qa-analyst` at validation | Originals, selected scope, accepted design, independent expectations, candidate and exact source/target state. | Separate numerical, interaction, visual, access, accessibility and performance results; defects and unobserved lanes. |

The lead consolidates findings; QA does not manufacture acceptance for the other
profiles. Product owner preserves the Northstar and actual choices. Branding,
storytelling, motion and design systems remain accountable for the chosen target
experience. Specialists cannot bypass A–F or grant installation, execution or
publication authority through a task packet.
