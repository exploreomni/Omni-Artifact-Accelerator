# Report discovery after A–C

Reuse supplied answers and the current analytical narrative. Ask a short batch of
material questions, not this entire list verbatim. Confirm that the requested
artifact is a native Report, an interactive dashboard/App, or separately selected
outputs. A request for “paginated” can mean very different delivery behavior.

- Who reads the report, what decision or operational obligation does it support,
  and what makes its delivery acceptable?
- Which outputs matter: print, PDF, Excel, in-product reading? Is one authoritative?
  For Excel, do readers need a visual facsimile or clean analyzable tables?
- What page size, orientation, units, margins, required headers/footers, page
  numbering, group/section structure or sample report must be preserved?
- Which filters/parameters, conditional sections, totals, subreports and sorting
  affect the layout? What should happen with no data or a very large result?
- Which brand website, supplied logo, font and assets are authoritative? Reuse
  approved assets. Confirm font availability and legibility in printed output.
- What locale, timezone, number/currency formats and data-as-of disclosure apply?
- Should existing Omni caching remain, or is a custom freshness/refresh policy
  wanted? Separate source freshness, query cache and frozen delivered results.
  Never add periodic refresh or cache bypass by default.
- Are schedules, recipient-specific output or distribution requested? Capture
  the requirement separately from permission to create subscriptions or send.
- What must match exactly, what may improve, and who can accept deviations?
  Capture actual acceptance ownership; leave it unassigned when unknown.

Record decisions and quotations in private notes and existing design-intent
requirements. Refer unresolved engine features to the builder before proposing
them. At E present three feasible concrete report directions at the same quality
bar, then preserve the actual user's choice. Do not substitute three themes for
three meaningful compositions or discard necessary report behavior for appearance.
