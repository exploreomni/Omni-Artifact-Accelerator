# Dimensional visualization craft

Use when current intent selects dimensional comparisons, interactive 3D, or a
designer-delegated treatment that benefits the task. This lesson also helps reject
depth when refined 2D communicates better. Reuse the
[D discovery](../../omni-product-owner/references/visual-experience-discovery.md)
answer; do not repeat it or choose a new product in a specialist handoff.

## Keep the deliverable and evidence clear

| Deliverable | What it supplies | What it does not establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dimensional chart styling | Depth/material/light around a declared quantitative encoding | A measured third variable, uncertainty or working 3D controls. |
| Interactive 3D chart/model | Useful inspect/select/view/reset behavior | Analytical correctness from realistic geometry alone. |
| Rendered illustration | Authorized explanatory or atmospheric image with declared fidelity/crop | A query-bound chart, precise geometry or measured scene. |
| Spatial analysis | Source-keyed positions, units, relationships and exact evidence | Precise coordinates inferred from names or decorative placement. |
| Recorded replay | Ordered source identities, times/positions and explicit missing intervals | Unobserved movement, causality or participation outside the recording. |

A separate image is illustration unless its analytical mapping and source are
actually established. Copying a chart-like picture into an App does not create a
working chart runtime. Source sketches and JSON specimens below contain no engine.

## Resolve the encoding before the material

| Pattern | Preserve | Poor fit / simpler alternative |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sculpted bars or columns | Shared declared scale and meaningful baseline; constant width/depth unless separately encoded; signs, zero, ties, units and distinct missingness. Compare the quantitative front edges, not projected volumes. | Perspective or occlusion makes ranking ambiguous; use aligned flat bars or a table. Non-additive measures do not become additive volumes. |
| Dimensional trends/ribbons | Ordered time and value axes, visible gaps, exact observations. Constant decorative thickness/depth; smoothing never invents observations or confidence intervals. | Sparse or irregular observations where a continuous ribbon implies continuity; use points/segments with gaps. |
| Layered comparisons | Common scale, stable series identity/order, readable overlap and selected detail. Separate actual, target and forecast meanings in labels, not lighting alone. | Dense layers conceal values; use small multiples, paired dots or selected-pair comparison. |
| Interactive 3D chart | Named task, useful default analytical view, selected identity, purposeful alternate views, explicit reset and exact list/table route. Ordinary refresh preserves selection/camera when still valid. | Rotation is the only novelty or hides important marks; keep a fixed analytical view or refined 2D. |

Resolve the default view for the actual task. A front or orthographic view often
supports comparison; it is not a universal camera requirement. Correct tooltips do
not repair misleading perspective. Exact values must remain available without
hover, free rotation or a canvas-only action. Missing values are labels/states, not
zero-height observations or oversized numeric glyphs.

## Art direction is configurable

Use the user's brand and task to choose relationships among material, light, depth,
type and motion. The following are transferable proposals, not style presets:

| Direction | Useful relationship | Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mineral ledger | Warm light surface, restrained relief and dark precise labels make signed comparisons deliberate. | Metallic highlights that obscure sign or turn constant depth into apparent magnitude. |
| Signal trace | Dark instrument field, selective luminous edges and quiet labels focus attention on sequence and gaps. | Neon everywhere, glow as uncertainty, hidden axes or perpetual animation. |
| Porcelain layers | Pale planes, sparse color and distinct line grammars separate actual/target/forecast. | Translucency that changes semantic color or implies a confidence band. |
| Technical specimen | Restrained wire/solid hierarchy and named front/detail views support inspection. | Decorative orbiting, invented dimensions or a universal room/stadium shell. |

Refined 2D may use the same hierarchy, semantics and interaction without any depth.
Compare treatments using equal evidence and ambition; do not make the flat option
deliberately weaker or force “2D / dimensional / 3D” as the three product concepts.

## Resolve the selected visual

Extend the existing design/query handoff, without a new schema or lifecycle:

- Task, supported narrative claim, fields, grain, population, units, scales and source era.
- Chosen surface, mark/geometry mapping, material/light semantics, labels and exact-value route.
- Controls, useful views/camera where applicable, selection/return, resize and export behavior.
- Applicable loading, partial, empty, stale, restricted, failed, recovery and reduced-motion states; no old claim survives an invalidated required input.
- Runtime/dependency evidence, accessible fallback, observable acceptance and explicit remaining difference from the request.

Designers own chart composition; analysts/BI own numbers and binding. Bring in the
modeler only for justified geometry/object hierarchy/fidelity, and motion design
for meaningful transitions or replay. Engineering owns qualified rendering and
lifecycle. Preserve the selected native surface: an App specimen cannot establish
dashboard support or authorize a surface switch.

## Review the affected obligations

Independently check scales and exact values using mixed signs, zero, ties, missing
observations, long labels and relevant dense series. Under permitted observation,
inspect perspective, occlusion, labels, narrow/short space, keyboard selection,
exact evidence, reset and return. Reduced motion must preserve the same task and
values. Playback needs separate start/pause/completion/reset/interruption evidence.

Bind source, local numerical, rendered, native, effective-access and participant
findings to the exact candidate. Source-only specimens do not close the other
lanes. Use the existing four candidate reviews; correct the finding and retest its
affected obligation. Keep the shared dependency rules: no Blender, no unapproved
package/engine/font import or install, and no browser/headless workaround under a
CLI/API-only constraint. A treatment choice does not supply execution authority.
