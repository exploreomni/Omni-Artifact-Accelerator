# Omni Domo Migrator

Own Domo source expertise within the [Artifact Accelerator](../omni-artifact-accelerator/SKILL.md).
Resume the existing engagement, Domo manager/run and source selection. Direct
invocation enters that same workflow; do not establish another coordinator or
replace independent QA. The originating manager retains its store-backed decisions
and delivery gates.

Use the [Domo run guide](../omni-artifact-translator/references/domo-visual-run.md)
for executable capability and the [Domo bridge](../omni-artifact-translator/references/domo-agent-bridge.md)
for accepted contracts and producer pins. The bridge's four passive contracts do
not by themselves acquire images, execute Card queries or convert Apps. Vendor
documentation and available tools are not proof of a successfully exercised route.
Follow the shared [dependency policy](../omni-artifact-accelerator/references/dependency-policy.md);
this skill adds no runtime, credential, source-query or target-write authority.

## Establish the real scope

Recover the selected tenant, source principal/personas, Page/App/Card identities,
capture state, intended Omni surface and preserve-versus-improve intent. Reuse
existing source evidence before asking for recapture. Distinguish Cards from their
placements and App Studio components from custom Apps, Bricks and operational
dependencies. Establish the selected-object denominator independently of successful
query translation; missing, hidden, restricted and unsupported items stay visible.

Read only the references relevant to the selected work:

- [Source competencies](references/source-competencies.md): responsibility and
  evidence handoffs across discovery, interpretation and acceptance.
- [Visual semantics and mapping](references/visual-semantics.md): chart meaning,
  summary metrics, formulas, grain, totals, repeated placements and controls.
- [App behavior](references/app-behavior.md): App Studio, custom Apps, Bricks,
  persistent stores, workflows and explicit read-only versus write behavior.
- [Acquisition, security and QA](references/acquisition-security-qa.md): operation
  qualification, original files, matched-state comparisons and release boundaries.

## Preserve the sequence

All new engagements follow [A–F](../omni-artifact-accelerator/references/stage-gates.md),
including faithful migration. Verify access; analyze the data and source logic;
form a traceable narrative; interview for the artifact; present exactly three
concrete visual options with the same scope and quality ambition; record the actual
user choice and resolve detailed design before target coding. Source discovery and
passive capture can precede the choice; they cannot authorize a target build.
Resume historical engagements under their recorded policy rather than inventing
missing decisions.

At D retain audience, task, quality references, customer website and approved brand
assets, delivery contexts, supported interactions and target surface. Ask about the
desired target App [cache, refresh and optional expiry policy](../omni-product-owner/references/data-refresh-policy.md);
an explicit no-custom-policy answer is valid. Carry the resolved policy through E
and F. Source cache/freshness is evidence about the comparison population, not a
default target refresh policy.

Choose reuse, semantic reconstruction or warehouse refactoring explicitly. Reuse
verified Omni fields/Topics when they satisfy the accepted behavior; route actual
formula, grain, join, freshness or access gaps through the existing
[DMA exchange](../omni-backend-handoff/references/cowork-contract.md). Preserve the
Domo source route, originals and independent cases. Do not fabricate a DMA producer
contract, guess Beast Mode semantics or duplicate the Domo engine's control plane.

## Return an accountable migration slice

Give the translator original source IDs/bytes, independent coverage, per-placement
behavior and explicit mappings; give the engineers accepted query/experience
contracts; give QA original expectations and matched-state cases. Keep dashboard,
native App and native Report targets distinct. A source export format does not
select the target surface or qualify that target's authoring API.

An App may be entirely read-only. Record that disposition without inventing a
workflow, writeback or external side effect to satisfy an assignment schema.
Unrepresentable source work remains a source-contract gap. Persistent stores and
operational actions need separate architecture, ownership and authorization; they
are not promised native Omni equivalents.

Return evidence and remaining obligations to the originating manager. Distinguish
source captured, design selected, candidate prepared, native read back, independently
verified, accepted and published. Hashes prove local consistency, not source
authenticity, effective access, approval or completed migration. No publication or
source retirement is added by this specialist.
